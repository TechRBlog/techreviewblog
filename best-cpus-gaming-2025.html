❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Should I buy AMD or Intel for gaming in 2025?

AMD currently has the edge for pure gaming, especially with the 7800X3D. Intel is competitive but runs hotter and uses more power. For most people, I'd recommend AMD.

Is the Ryzen 7 7800X3D worth the extra cost over the 7600X?

If you have a high-end GPU (RTX 4080 or better) and game at 1080p/1440p with high refresh rates, yes. The performance difference is significant in CPU-limited scenarios.

What about Intel's new 15th gen CPUs?

Intel's 15th gen isn't expected until late 2025. The current 14th gen is solid but AMD's offerings provide better value and efficiency right now.

Do I need 16 cores for gaming?

No, most games use 4-8 cores effectively. 16 cores are beneficial if you stream, create content, or run background applications while gaming.
