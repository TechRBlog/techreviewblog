Why Standing Desks Matter for Your Health

Medical research consistently shows that prolonged sitting increases risks of cardiovascular disease, diabetes, and premature death. Standing desks offer proven health benefits including:

  • Reduced back pain: 89% of users report significant pain reduction within 4 weeks
  • Better blood sugar control: Standing after meals reduces spikes by up to 43%
  • Increased productivity: 15% boost in focus and alertness
  • Enhanced mood: Standing increases endorphin production
  • Improved posture: Strengthens core muscles and spine alignment
